How to listen

Press Sound key first. Each frame sweeps once across the world, west (left ear) to east (right ear).

  • Pitch is latitude. North is high, south is low.
  • Loudness is intensity. Silence means no data or dry.
  • Pluck is rain or temperature. Bell is snow.
  • In cycle mode, one latitude band plays per sweep.
